# AI Warroom 90

A 90-day intensive transition from senior backend engineering (.NET, 14 years) to production AI engineering. Daily public commits, building in public.

## Background

I'm a software engineer with 14 years of backend experience, currently in commodity intelligence at S&P Global. I'm using AI engineering as my next specialization — focused on production-grade systems (RAG, agents, LLM integration), not academic ML.

## Why public

Building in public for accountability and to share the journey honestly — including what breaks, what I don't yet understand, and what I'd do differently.

## Structure

- **Phase 1 (Days 1-30):** Foundations. LLM APIs, prompt engineering, embeddings, structured outputs. Daily small builds.
- **Phase 2 (Days 31-60):** Multi-step systems. RAG, agents, tool use, memory, backend integration.
- **Phase 3 (Days 61-90):** Production architecture. Performance, evaluation, deployment, system design.

## Daily commitment

4 hours/day. Daily commits. Weekly major projects. No tutorials without shipping.

Commodity intelligence — connecting AI engineering capability to my domain at S&P Global (commodity research, market commentary, regulatory data).
